# Branding

- **Name:** Akceo. The command is `akceo`.
- **Domain:** akceo.ai

## Naming rationale

The name moves through three steps.

- **Actio** is "the act of doing, a driving force, or delivery". It is grounded in movement and
  physical execution.
- **Akcio** is a sharp, geometric spelling of Actio that looks like a cutting-edge AI or automated
  tool.
- **Akceo**: changing that one vowel does something incredibly powerful for a software brand: it
  instantly connects your rapid deck builder to the Chief Executive Officer (CEO). Since
  presentation decks are the ultimate tool for corporate leaders, startup founders, and pitch
  masters, Akceo subtly tells the user: "This tool makes your slides look like they were built by
  a high-level executive."

By shifting from Akcio to Akceo, you also change the physical meaning. Instead of just meaning
"action" or "delivery," it now means "Executive Action" or "Commanding Delivery." It sounds
prestigious, fast, and highly authoritative.
